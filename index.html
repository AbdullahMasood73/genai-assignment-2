Overall outcome rules

    Label meanings

    • included / not included – inclusion criterion met / not met.
    • excluded / not excluded – disqualifying condition present / absent.
    • not enough information – the record cannot resolve the criterion.
    • not applicable – the criterion does not apply to this patient.

    Limitations

    The model is a small fine-tuned Pythia decoder trained on 37 patients. Its labels and evidence citations are frequently wrong (see report). Explanations are templates built from the predicted label and the verbatim cited sentences; they are not free-text clinical reasoning.
